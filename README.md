## Dear Unity Developers
My name is GH Games, I'm like batman but not cool and have nothing to show for myself.

After using Unity for 8 years I have switched to Godot. I used to get a thrill from making something in Unity. It felt like I had the power to create whatever I wanted, even though I have a habit of never finishing a project.

Unity admittedly helped me refine my C# skills, become addicted to making Shaders, fall in love with lighting techniques and that damn Volumetric Fog. It enabled me to forge a workflow that is very dynamic and adaptable to any kind of project I wanted to start.

Switching to Godot, all that experience still applies very well, I just have to use a different mindset and a node based approach. Which I'm loving and honestly doesn't feel that much different to Unity's component system.

That thrill I used to get from Unity, I now get from Godot.

### Godot feels like what Unity felt like 8 years ago.
